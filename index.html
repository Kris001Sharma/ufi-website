Manufacturing Excellence

The 9-Step Powder Coating Process

Our signature finish guarantees decades of rust-free durability. Every piece of UFI furniture undergoes this rigorous chemical and thermal treatment before it leaves the factory floor.

01

Degreasing

Removes oil, grease and dirt from the raw metal surface.

02

Water Rinse 1

Rinses off the cleaning agent before the next stage.

03

Derusting / Pickling

Removes rust and scale to expose clean, bare metal.

04

Water Rinse 2

Rinses off residual acid from the pickling stage.

05

Neutralizing

Neutralizes any remaining acid reaction on the surface.

06

Water Rinse 3

Rinses off neutralizing residues before phosphating.

07

Phosphating

Creates a protective phosphate layer so the coating bonds permanently.

08

Water Rinse 4

A final rinse with clean water before passivation.

09

Passivation

Enhances corrosion resistance ahead of the powder coating booth.
